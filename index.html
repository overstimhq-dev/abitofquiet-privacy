A Bit of Quiet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

App and developer information

This privacy policy applies to A Bit of Quiet, a wellbeing app designed to provide quiet routines, check-ins, coping ideas, calming sounds and related tools.

Privacy enquiries and data-protection requests can be sent to a.bit.of.quiet1@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The app can store information you choose to enter or create, including check-in and mood selections, energy and overstimulation ratings, notes, routines, transition profiles, preferred strategies, breathing and sound preferences, theme and accessibility preferences, reminder settings, Tiny Step feedback and progress counts, and other app settings.

This information is stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not use automatic cloud synchronisation.

Health and wellbeing information

Some information you enter may reveal information about your mental or physical wellbeing. Because of the nature of the app, this can include sensitive personal information. A Bit of Quiet does not connect to Android Health Connect or other health-record systems and does not import health information from other apps.

Tiny Step and clipboard

Tiny Step generates its suggestions on your device. The app does not send your Tiny Step task, check-in history, notebook, routines or other saved records to an external service.

If you choose Use clipboard text, the app reads the current text on your device clipboard so it can place that text into the Tiny Step task field. Clipboard text is not automatically read when Tiny Step opens, is not sent to a server by the app, and is not stored as a separate clipboard record. You should avoid using clipboard text that contains passwords, payment details or other information you do not want placed into the task field.

Backups and sharing

You can manually export an A Bit of Quiet backup as a JSON file. A backup can contain the information described above, including sensitive information you have entered. The app then uses your device’s sharing tools only when you choose to share the backup.

A Bit of Quiet does not automatically upload backups or send them to a service. Once you choose to send or store a backup outside the app, the recipient or service you choose controls how that copy is handled.

Notifications, sound and device features

If you enable reminders, A Bit of Quiet can schedule local notifications on your device. The app also uses local audio playback and vibration for calming, breathing and support features.

The app does not request microphone recording permission and does not use your camera, contacts or precise location. The app does not control your device’s screen brightness; brightness remains under your control.

Advertising, analytics and third-party services

A Bit of Quiet does not contain personalised advertising and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current release.

The current app features do not send your saved app data to a remote service. Device operating-system services may process information under their own terms when you use normal Android features such as notifications or the system share sheet.

How information is protected

Saved app data is kept in the app’s local storage. For supported records, the app has a secure-storage fallback using the platform’s secure storage facility. The app does not maintain a remote copy of your saved data in the current release.

Retention and deletion

Your saved app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or remove the app’s stored data or uninstall the app. A Bit of Quiet provides a Delete all saved data option in Settings. This clears the app’s saved local data and returns the app to its initial state.

Exported backup files are separate files and remain wherever you chose to store them until you delete them. Copies shared with other people or services are outside the app’s control.

Your choices and rights

You choose whether to enter information, enable reminders, use clipboard text, create backups or use the app’s optional features. Because the current release does not create user accounts or maintain a remote copy of your saved app data, there is no online account to delete. Under applicable UK data-protection law, you may have rights relating to personal data, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection, depending on the circumstances. Privacy requests can be made using the contact details above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change or when required for legal or platform-policy reasons. The latest version will be made available through the published privacy-policy page and reflected in the app where appropriate.

A Bit of Quiet is intended to support everyday wellbeing. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice or emergency assistance. If you need urgent help, contact your local emergency services.
