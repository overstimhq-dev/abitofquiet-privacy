A Bit of Quiet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who this policy is for

This policy explains how A Bit of Quiet handles information when you use the app. A Bit of Quiet is a wellbeing app designed to provide quiet routines, check-ins, coping ideas, calming sounds and related tools. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose conditions and is not an emergency service.

Information stored by the app

The app can store information you choose to enter, including mood and check-in selections, energy and overstimulation ratings, notes, routines, transition profiles, preferred strategies, breathing and sound preferences, theme settings and other app preferences.

This information is intended to stay on your device. The app does not require an account and does not use automatic cloud synchronisation.

Health and wellbeing information

Some information entered into the app may reveal information about your mental or physical wellbeing. We treat this as sensitive information. The app does not connect to Health Connect or other health-record systems and does not collect health information from other apps.

Optional external service

A Bit of Quiet contains an optional dynamic “Tiny Step” feature that can be configured to use an external HTTPS service. If that service is enabled, the text of the task you submit for that feature and a small step number are sent to that service so it can return one suggested micro-step.

The app does not send your full check-in history, notebook, routines or other saved records as part of that request. You should avoid entering sensitive personal information into an external-service prompt.

Backups and sharing

You can manually export a backup of your app data as a JSON file and use your device’s sharing tools to move it. A backup can contain sensitive information you have entered into the app. Once you choose to share or store a backup outside the app, it is subject to the privacy and security practices of the service or person you share it with.

Notifications, sound and device features

If you enable reminders, the app can schedule local notifications on your device. The app also uses local audio playback, vibration and, where supported and permitted, temporary screen-brightness adjustment for its calming and emergency-support features.

The app does not request microphone recording permission and does not use your camera, contacts or precise location.

Advertising and analytics

A Bit of Quiet does not contain personalised advertising and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current release.

How information is protected

Saved app data is stored locally using the app’s local storage mechanisms, with a secure-storage fallback for supported records. Network requests for the optional external service use HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

The app keeps your saved information on the device until you delete it through the app or remove the app’s stored data/uninstall the app. Exported backups are separate files and remain wherever you choose to store them until you delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to enter information, enable reminders, use the optional external service and create backups. Under applicable UK data-protection law, you may have rights relating to your personal data, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection, depending on the circumstances.

Privacy contact

Privacy enquiries and data-protection requests can be sent to a.bit.of.quiet1@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change or when required for legal or platform-policy reasons. The latest version will be made available with the app and through the published privacy-policy page.

A Bit of Quiet is intended to support everyday wellbeing. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice or emergency assistance.
